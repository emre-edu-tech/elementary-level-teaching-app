# 08 — Turkish Content & Lexicon

Source of truth for ALL learning content. Implement as `app/lexicon.py`
(Python, used by server for STT matching) and expose the parts the frontend
needs via the `play.html` config injection (as JSON in `window.GAME_CONFIG.lexicon`).

## Numbers

Digits as digits: `"7"`, `"21"`. Words:

- 1–10: bir, iki, üç, dört, beş, altı, yedi, sekiz, dokuz, on
- Tens: 20 yirmi, 30 otuz, 40 kırk, 50 elli, 60 altmış, 70 yetmiş,
  80 seksen, 90 doksan, 100 yüz
- 11–19: `on` + unit ("on bir"). 21–99: tens word + space + unit
  ("kırk üç"). Turkish number words are fully regular — no exceptions.

`numberWord(n)`: implement in lexicon.py for 1–100 per rules above; raise on
out-of-range. Used for TTS instructions and STT matching.

STT accepted set for target `n` (exact strings AFTER normalization from 10):
`{str(n), numberWord(n) with spaces removed}` — e.g. for 21:
`{"21", "yirmibir"}`. Note TDK writes "yirmi bir" with a space, and Whisper
may output either or joined — the space-stripping in normalization handles
both, so store/compare space-free.

## Letters (29) — names + STT variants

Turkish alphabet order. Columns: Letter | TTS name (what is spoken) | accepted
STT variants (pre-normalization; normalization removes spaces/punctuation and
maps ı→i, so "yumuşak ge" → "yumasakge").

```
A a | a           | a
B b | be          | be, beh, b
C c | ce          | ce, ceh, c
Ç ç | çe          | çe, çeh, ç
D d | de          | de, deh, d
E e | e           | e
F f | fe          | fe, feh, f
G g | ge          | ge, geh, g
Ğ ğ | yumuşak ge  | yumusakge, yumusakgeh, yumusak ge
H h | he          | he, heh, h
I ı | ı           | ı
İ i | i           | i
J j | je          | je, jeh, j
K k | ke          | ke, keh, k
L l | le          | le, leh, l
M m | me          | me, meh, m
N n | ne          | ne, neh, n
O o | o           | o
Ö ö | ö           | ö
P p | pe          | pe, peh, p
R r | re          | re, reh, r
S s | se          | se, seh, s
Ş ş | şe          | şe, şeh, ş
T t | te          | te, teh, t
U u | u           | u
Ü ü | ü           | ü
V v | ve          | ve, veh, v
Y y | ye          | ye, yeh, y
Z z | zed         | zed, zedh, z
```

Notes:
- `I ı` vs `İ i` are distinct letters — keep them distinct through
  normalization (normalization maps BOTH ı and i to `i`; this is intentional
  and safe because the app never asks voice answers for vowels — see 07).
- Matching adds Levenshtein ≤ 1 on top of exact variant match (rule in 10).

## Frontend data

`window.GAME_CONFIG.lexicon` carries: `numberWords` (1–100 map, string→string),
`letters` (array of `{char, lower, name}` in alphabet order). Generated by
Jinja from lexicon.py — single source of truth, no duplicated content in JS.
